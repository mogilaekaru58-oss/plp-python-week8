FINAL PROJECT: PERSONAL MINI-TOOLKIT

1. TOOL: To-Do List Manager
This tool lets users add, view, and remove tasks while the program is running.
Concepts: lists, loops, conditionals, input/output, and f-strings.

2. TOOL: Simple Calculator
This tool lets users add, subtract, multiply, and divide two numbers.
Concepts: variables, input, conditionals, arithmetic operators, and f-strings.

3. TOOL: Even and Odd Number Checker
This tool checks whether a number is even or odd.
Concepts: input, integer conversion, conditionals, the modulo operator, and f-strings.
